MADQA system architecture

How our open-weight agentic RAG answers a question today, what each part does, and which parts are still being tested. Snapshot from 7 November 2026.

Best scores: 94.5 on the 200 dev questions (189 right, judged at a score of 0.5 or higher, with ColQwen; reruns land at 188 to 189, and dev has been used for tuning). The project log's stricter count was 91.0 before ColQwen. On 200 train questions never used for tuning (weighted to the test mix) the stack scored 92.0 in the log (185 right, 92.5, by the looser count) and ColQwen did not change that sample; over all 1,550 train questions ColQwen fixed 30 and broke 3 (about +1.7 points). Expect about 93 on the test mix. Qwen 3.8 only. (Adding Gemma 4 31B as a second reader reached 92.5 and 92.0 but isn't used.) Verify alone scores 88.0 on train. The judge is DeepSeek rather than the leaderboard's Gemini, and nothing has been submitted, so there's no official score yet. Targets: 94.4 best open-weight, 96.9 overall.
